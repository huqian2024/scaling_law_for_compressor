## Why this archive exists

`analysis/geometry_review/` was rebuilt for **V2 (exploratory extended ranges)**.
The V1 review (legacy ranges only) produced real results that must not be lost, so
its metadata, results and figures were copied here before the V2 rebuild.

## Known gap in this archive (recorded honestly)

```text
tools/plot_geometry_review.py (V1 version) was NOT copied here before the V2
rewrite replaced it in place. It was an untracked file, so it is not
recoverable from git.
```

What this means in practice:

- the V1 **figures** (the actual deliverable) are archived under `figures/`;
- the V1 **data tools** (`make_review_cases.py`, `make_review_summary.py`) are
  archived here;
- only the V1 *plotting script* is missing; it is superseded by the V2 version,
  and every V1 figure can be regenerated by pointing the V2 script at this
  archive with `--cases` / `--geometry` / `--qc` overrides.
